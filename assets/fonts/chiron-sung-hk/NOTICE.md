# Chiron Sung HK webfont

Source package: `chiron-sung-hk-webfont@1.0.24` from the npm registry.
Font version: 1.024. Family: `Chiron Sung HK WS`.
Project: https://github.com/chiron-fonts/chiron-sung-hk

The unmodified normal variable CSS and its WOFF2 subsets are included. Italic files are not installed. Redistribution licence and copyright are in `LICENSE.md`.

CalCal keeps Times New Roman for English and uses Chiron Sung HK WS as its Chinese fallback. Setting the document language to Chinese selects Chiron first. Font settings are in `css/fonts.css` at the app root.
